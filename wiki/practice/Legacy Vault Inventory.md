## Restartability Assets

Most serious legacy domains already include:

- `专题总览.md`
- `学习进度.md`
- `恢复笔记.md`

That means the migration should not start by summarizing every file. Start each domain from these three notes, then promote only the highest-value maps, playbooks, and system notes.

## Suggested Promotion Order

1. `AI-Engineering/`: directly supports Hermes, Codex, ECC, engineering agents, evaluation, and delivery workflows.
2. `AI-Open-Source/`: directly supports the open-source research system and tool watchlists.
3. `AI-Learning/`: contains broad AI systems, companies, products, papers, and maps.
4. `AI-Applications/` and `AI-Architect/`: convert to practical playbooks and architecture comparisons.
5. Non-AI support domains: promote only when they support active AI engineering or business workflows.
